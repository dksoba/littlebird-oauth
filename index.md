# Littlebird

Littlebird is a personal, single-user tool that summarizes the owner's own
school emails on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

## Privacy policy

- Littlebird requests read-only Gmail access (`gmail.readonly`). It never
  sends, deletes, labels, or marks email as read.
- Email is processed only on the owner's computer, by a local language
  model. No email content is sent to any third-party service.
- Summaries and the sign-in token are stored only on that computer.
- No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
- Access can be revoked at any time at
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
